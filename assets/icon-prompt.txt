最终编辑提示词
Edit the provided square Cotex app-icon artwork. Retain the original single, front-facing open-book outline with two equal curved pages, one vertical central spine and smoothly rounded turns. Reduce the book so its outer black silhouette occupies approximately 71% of the square’s width and 48% of its height, centered both horizontally and vertically with generous, balanced white margins. Increase the actual black line weight to about 5.6% of the full canvas side, including the center spine, so it is visibly thicker than the reference even after the book is reduced. Keep the strokes uniform, crisp, smoothly curved and optically balanced at Windows icon sizes. Use solid black on a clean, solid white, opaque square background extending to the image edges. The rounded-square tile and transparent outer corners will be applied during export. No text, page marks, additional objects, borders, shadow, gradients or texture.
